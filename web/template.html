    How this works

    1. Library. Each PDF is cut into chunks of about 1,000 characters, a few paragraphs each.

    2. Retrieve. Your question is matched against every chunk with BM25 keyword scoring. Rare words that appear in both count the most. The bars show each chunk's score.

    3. Answer. Only the top passages and your question go to Claude, with an instruction to answer from them alone and cite them as [1], [2]. Click a citation to see its passage.
